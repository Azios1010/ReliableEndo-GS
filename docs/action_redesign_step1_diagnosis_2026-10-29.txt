ACTION REDESIGN, STEP 1: WHY THE FROZEN GAUSSIAN ACTION HURT THE RIGHT VIEW  (2026-10-29)
Status: EXPLORATORY diagnosis, read-only, no training, no gate. Sealed 64 frames and final_test untouched.
Evidence (scripts, raw results): ~/.local/share/reliableendo-gs/reports/action-redesign-20261029/
Frames: 24 val_7 frames outside the sealed 64 and the 56 benchmark IDs + 16 train frames (4 per dataset 1,2,3,6).

1. WHAT WAS MEASURED
   [DO] The earlier "left half helps both views, right half hurts the right view" pattern was a coarse view of something
   narrower. Applying the action to each cell of a 4x4 grid separately (640 cells, 40 frames), mean change of whole-image MSE
   (x1e-4; negative = better), cells improving the right view in brackets:
        grid column      left view     right view
        0 (x   0-160)     -0.47        -0.49  [86%]
        1 (x 160-320)     -1.00        -1.04  [89%]
        2 (x 320-480)     -0.87        -1.05  [92%]
        3 (x 480-640)     -1.12        +8.45  [ 6%]
   Full-frame action, change of squared error by image column (x1e-4, 80-px bins, mean over 40 frames):
        bin x range   baseline R   change R   change L
         0   0- 80       40.7       -14.3       -2.2
         1  80-160       42.8       -14.7      -12.7
         2 160-240       45.5       -17.4      -16.3
         3 240-320       45.9       -19.7      -16.4
         4 320-400       44.6       -20.0      -13.1
         5 400-480       34.1       -14.6      -15.6
         6 480-560       53.7       +14.5      -14.7
         7 560-640      190.4      +286.6      -21.4
   In the interior the right-view error falls 30-45%; the whole right-view loss comes from the last ~160 columns.

2. WHAT DOES NOT EXPLAIN IT
   [DO] Across cells, local disparity, depth error against GT, surface tilt relative to the left or right camera ray and the
   fraction of the cell visible in the right image explain almost none of the right-view harm (single-feature R2 <= 0.02).
   Horizontal image position alone: R2 0.20 (val), 0.26 (train) - i.e. the border band, not geometry.

3. A CHEAP GUARD ALREADY CHANGES THE PICTURE  (post-hoc K scan on the same frames: optimistic)
   Action applied to columns [0, W-K), change in PSNR vs STOP (dB), mean over frames:
        K (px)   val_7: left  right   both better     train: left  right   both better
          0             +2.97  -1.61     2/24                +1.52  -1.38     3/16
         40             +2.31  +1.09    24/24                +1.30  +1.22    15/16
         80             +2.07  +1.02    24/24                +1.26  +1.19    16/16
        160             +1.60  +0.84    24/24                +1.17  +1.13    16/16
   Leaving out only the last 40 columns (6% of the width) turns a trade-off into a gain in both views, in every val frame.

4. ROOT CAUSE CANDIDATE (strongest evidence so far)
   [DO] During Stage A all 52,600 training cores sat at exactly two horizontal positions (50% centred in x 80-160, 50% in
   x 400-480); none touched the last 160 columns. The action was trained on primitives from four fixed image positions and
   was never exposed to border primitives. The training objective was local (a 32x32 core, both views) and never measured
   the whole frame.
   [SUY LUAN] The harm is an extrapolation failure at the right border, not an inherent left/right trade-off. The exact
   mechanism at the border (e.g. low-support edge primitives amplified by the opacity/scale correction) is NOT yet
   identified; no test of it has been run.

5. LIMITS
   Exploratory, 40 frames, same frames used to pick K, in-sample for train frames, one checkpoint. These numbers justify a
   redesign; they are not a result and not a gate.

6. WHAT THIS IMPLIES FOR THE REDESIGN (proposal, nothing approved or trained yet)
   - Train the refinement over the WHOLE frame (all valid primitives, all positions incl. borders), not four fixed cores.
   - Objective: full-frame, both-view reconstruction loss, so border harm is penalised where it occurs.
   - Apply it inside the single render (before the first render): about +1 ms instead of the ~18 ms separate action pass.
   - Keep a visibility/support guard as an ablation, not as the main fix.
   - Training cost is small (frozen student, ~1.7k trainable parameters, one render pair per step).
   - Success bar and fresh confirmation frames must be set by the maintainer before any confirmatory run.
